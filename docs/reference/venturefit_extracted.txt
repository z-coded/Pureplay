 Abstract  
A huge number of startup failures are attributed to “market misreading,” where founders build 
products that solve non -existent problems due to a lack of structured market validation. 
Currently, aspiring entrepreneurs rely on fragmented tools, manually scou ring platforms such 
as Reddit for problem signals, collecting competitor data from multiple sources, and using 
generic business plan templates that fail to provide personalized guidance. This process is slow, 
unstructured, and often results in months of wa sted effort and resources. However, existing 
solutions do not provide an integrated environment that systematically converts real market 
discussions into actionable validation insights. VentureFit addresses this gap by consolidating 
the entire early -stage validation process into a single AI -powered workspace. By automatically 
mining discussions from open community platforms, applying thematic clustering to surface 
customer pain points, and performing sentiment -driven competitor gap analysis, the platform 
transforms scattered market signals into structured insights. The system further generates 
actionable reports that guide founders through idea validation, competitor analysis, customer 
discovery, launch planning, and go -to-market strategy development. By red ucing manual 
research effort and enabling data -driven decision making, VentureFit increases the likelihood 
of founders building products that address real market needs. The core analytical pipeline, 
comprising topic modeling, sentiment scoring, and behavio ral clustering, is built and executed 
in-house. LLM APIs are used solely for natural language report generation.

 Chapter 1: Introduction and Problem Definition  
This chapter situates VentureFit within the startup ecosystem, articulates the problem it 
addresses, defines the proposed solution and its objectives, surveys related systems, and 
specifies the tools and technologies that will underpin the implementation.  
1.1  Overview of the Project  
VentureFit  is an AI -powered, web -based startup validation platform built for first -time 
founders who need structured, evidence -based feedback on the viability of their ideas before 
committing time and capital to product development. The platform accepts a founder’s concept 
description and a set of seed keywords, then automatically retrieves relevant discussions from 
a 3M -record offline corpus via FAISS semantic search and from live community APIs (Reddit, 
Hacker News, and GitHub). These raw signals are processed thro ugh an entirely in -house NLP 
pipeline comprising sentence -transformer embeddings (all -MiniLM -L6-v2), heuristic 
complaint scoring, and HDBSCAN -based thematic clustering to surface validated pain points, 
score idea -market fit, map competitor weaknesses, and construct customer personas. LLM 
APIs (DeepSeek, Groq, HuggingFace) are used in Module 2 for query expansion and topic title 
generation, and in the later stages of the pipeline (Modules 6 through 8) exclusively to generate 
the prose of strategic reports; t hey play no role in analysis, scoring, or clustering.  
The platform is delivered as a Next.js web application backed by a FastAPI service and a 
PostgreSQL database. Its nine modules take a founder from raw idea intake through to a 
downloadable Startup Validation Dossier, replacing what would otherwise be weeks  of 
fragmented manual research with an automated pipeline that runs in the background and 
presents findings through an interactive dashboard.  
1.2  Vision Statement  
For first-time founders who struggle to validate their startup ideas and navigate the messy early 
stages of market research, the VentureFit  is an AI -powered validation platform that 
transforms a raw concept into a data -backed, actionable roadmap. Unlike  fragmented tools that 
leave founders to piece together insights manually, our product  automatically retrieves and 
mines real community discussions via open APIs, scores ideas against market signals, maps 
competitors via live scraping and datasets, and generates  customized go -to-market strategies 
dramatically increasing the probability of building something people actually want.  
1.3  Problem Statement  
Many startup founders struggle to determine whether their business idea addresses a genuine 
and significant market problem. Without structured validation methods, entrepreneurs often 
rely on personal assumptions, intuition, or feedback from a small group o f individuals. This 
approach frequently results in the development of products that do not meet real customer 
needs. Currently, validating a startup idea requires founders to manually research online 
discussions, analyze user complaints, identify competito rs, and interpret scattered market 
signals from various sources. This process is time -consuming and requires significant effort to 
identify patterns and meaningful insights from unstructured data. In many cases, entrepreneurs 
lack the technical expertise n eeded to perform sentiment analysis, trend identification, or 
structured market research. As a result, founders may misunderstand customer problems, 
overlook potential opportunities, or underestimate the level of competition in the market. 
Existing tools s uch as survey platforms, discussion forums, and business planning templates

1.4  Problem Solution  
VentureFit acts as an intelligent co -pilot for early -stage startup validation. The platform ingests 
a founder's initial idea and systematically gathers evidence from multiple sources to assess 
viability. It then presents all findings through an intuitive d ashboard, guiding the founder 
through each stage of validation: problem discovery, idea scoring, competitor analysis, 
customer identification, launch planning, and growth strategy design. By automating data 
collection and analysis, VentureFit reduces valid ation time from months to days.  
1.5  Objectives of the Proposed System  
BO-1: Reduce founder market research time significantly through automated data collection 
and AI -driven thematic synthesis.  
BO-2: Provide quantitative evidence of problem -solution fit using real user discussions, 
sentiment analysis, and volume -trend scoring.  
BO-3: Identify at least three distinct customer segments with corresponding needs, 
demographics, and channel preferences.  
BO-4: Generate a competitive gap analysis highlighting unmet needs and differentiation 
opportunities using curated datasets and live scraping.  
BO-5: Deliver a customized MVP scope, phased timeline, and budget estimate based on idea 
complexity and team parameters.  
BO-6: Produce a data -backed go -to-market strategy with channel recommendations and 
tailored messaging.  
BO-7: Equip founders with a retention and growth framework to acquire and retain the first 
100 users.  
BO-8: Create a reusable validation knowledge base that evolves as the founder refines their 
idea.  
1.6  Scope  
VentureFit is a web -based platform comprising a backend processing engine and a frontend 
interactive dashboard. The backend handles all data acquisition, NLP processing, and analysis, 
while the frontend provides a seamless experience for idea intake and vi sualisations. The 
system utilizes structured database storage and a task queue for managing long -running 
background analysis. The platform is strictly scoped to early -stage validation and does not 
include post -launch features like fundraising tracking or l ive deployment. The intelligence 
foundation relies on four data sources: a 3M -record FAISS offline index for semantic retrieval; 
live community APIs (Reddit native search, PullPush, and Arctic Shift for Reddit; Hacker 
News Algolia; GitHub Issues) for real -time discussions; curated competitor datasets enhanced 
by live scraping of public pages; and LLM APIs for query expansion, topic title generation,

clustering, and scoring) runs on locally executed models.  
1.6.1  Limitations and Constraints  
LI-1: Problem discovery quality depends on the volume of relevant discussions retrievable via 
open community APIs; niche markets may yield sparse results, mitigated by the FAISS offline 
index and relaxed re -fetching from live sources.  
LI-2: Competitor analysis is aid -oriented and not an exhaustive market intelligence tool.  
LI-3: Dependence on LLM APIs (DeepSeek, Groq, HuggingFace) for query expansion and 
topic title generation in Module 2, and report generation in Modules 6 –8; deterministic 
template fallbacks are implemented for all LLM outages.  
LI-4: Customer segmentation is data -informed and approximate, not individually verified.  
LI-5: Timeline and budget estimates are structured guides based on industry averages.  
LI-6: NLP processing is currently constrained to English -language content only.  
LI-7: Live scraping is limited to publicly accessible pages; anti -scraping measures may revert 
the system to cached or fallback datasets.  
LI-8: Fallback dataset usage may lack real -time "trend" accuracy compared to live API 
signals.  
LI-9: The prototype demonstration operates on data retrieved at evaluation time via open 
community discussion APIs. The NLP and clustering pipeline is generalized and not restricted 
to specific ideas  output quality for any given concept scales directly wi th the volume of relevant 
discussions available through these sources.  
1.7  Modules  
VentureFit is composed of nine functional modules, each scoped to a distinct stage of the 
validation workflow.  
Module 1.7.1: User Onboarding and Idea Intake  
● FE-1: Secure user registration and login to establish isolated workspaces.  
● FE-2: Project creation interface for inputting startup idea details, target industry, and 
keywords.  
● FE-3: Secure storage of user data and project history to maintain multiple simultaneous 
projects.  
● FE-4: Automatic trigger of the validation pipeline via background task queuing.  
Module 1.7.2: Problem Discovery Engine  
● FE-1: A Dual -Path Retrieval system that performs semantic search over a 3M -record 
FAISS offline index using LLM -expanded queries and concurrently fetches live 
discussions from Reddit (native search, PullPush, Arctic Shift), Hacker News Algolia, and 
GitHub Issue s using expanded keyword tokens from the project context.  
● FE-2: A unified NLP preprocessing pipeline that performs tokenization, stopword 
removal, and normalization on all incoming raw text from both sources to ensure data 
consistency for machine learning analysis.  
● FE-3: An advanced Thematic Clustering process that applies HDBSCAN -based density 
clustering (with Agglomerative and KMeans fallbacks) on all -MiniLM -L6-v2 sentence -
transformer embeddings to group retrieved complaints into coherent, actionable problem 
themes.

1.8  Related System Analysis and Literature Review  
1.8.1  Literature Review  
Prior research on early -stage market validation consistently identifies two failure modes. 
Founders either skip validation entirely or rely on tools that are too narrow in scope to provide 
meaningful insights. The Lean Startup methodology proposed by Ries in 2011 offers a 
framework for iterative, evidence -based product development, but it assumes access to real 
customer feedback, a resource that first -time founders often do not have. More recent work by 
Wei and colleagues in 2022 demonstrates that large lan guage models can support chain -of-
thought reasoning for generating strategic text, which informs VentureFit’s deliberate use of 
LLM APIs in the later modules.  
The technical foundation of VentureFit’s analytics is drawn directly from established NLP 
research. Sentence -BERT, introduced by Reimers and Gurevych in 2019, demonstrates that 
fine-tuned transformer models produce semantically meaningful sentence embeddin gs suitable 
for similarity search, which underpins VentureFit’s use of the all -MiniLM -L6-v2 model for 

--- Page 14 ---
 
6 
 both FAISS retrieval and clustering. HDBSCAN, introduced by McInnes and colleagues in 
2017, is a density -based clustering algorithm that handles variable -density clusters and noise 
without requiring a fixed cluster count, making it well -suited for the unev en distribution of 
complaint topics found in real community discussions.  
Existing tools and platforms fail to integrate these capabilities. Manual browsing of online 
communities is unstructured and slow. Lean Canvas and Strategyzer provide frameworks for 
planning but do not integrate data. SurveyMonkey requires a pre -existing a udience. VentureFit 
addresses this gap by providing an automated, end -to-end pipeline that combines discussion 
mining, NLP clustering, competitor analysis, and strategic report generation specifically 
designed for early -stage founders.

1.10  Project Contribution  
VentureFit  makes several concrete technical and conceptual contributions that distinguish it 
from existing solutions.  
• Custom -Built NLP Pipeline: The analytical core (Modules 2 –5) is implemented entirely 
in-house using sentence -transformer embeddings (all -MiniLM -L6-v2) for semantic 
search and clustering, HDBSCAN for thematic clustering, and scikit -learn for 
clustering fall backs. LLM APIs are used for query expansion and topic title generation 
in Module 2 and scoped to report text generation in Modules 6 –8. 
• Integrated  End-to-End Validation : Combines  discovery,  analysis,  and planning  into 
a single  guided  pipeline.  
• Automated Thematic Clustering: Applies HDBSCAN on sentence -transformer 
embeddings to surface latent pain points, with volume - and engagement -weighted 
severity scoring.  
• Dual -Layer  Data  Acquisition : Implements high -availability logic combining a 3M -
record FAISS offline index with live community API fetchers (Reddit, Hacker News, 
GitHub), supporting hybrid, faiss_only, and online_only retrieval modes.  
• Behavioral  Segment  Generation : Uses  clustering  on metadata  to build  grounded  
founder  personas.  
• Hybrid  Competitor  Analysis : Merges static datasets with live scraping for real -time 
market void detection.  
Contribution Impact : 
VentureFit  delivers a unified, automated pipeline for early -stage startup validation, combining 
community discussion mining, NLP analysis, competitor assessment, and strategic report 
generation. By providing actionable insights, behavioral personas, and data -driven 
recommendations, it accelerates validation from months to days. The platform reduces risk for 
first-time founders, improves decision -making, and offers a scalable, reliable approach to 
assessing idea viability.

2.2  Operating Environment  
OE-1: The system shall operate as a web -based application accessible through modern 
desktop browsers, including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Safari 
(current and one prior stable version), over an HTTPS connection.  
OE-2: The backend system shall operate on a Linux -based server environment, such as 
Ubuntu 22.04 LTS or an equivalent distribution, with sufficient processing power, 
memory, and storage to support application and database operations.  
OE-3: The system should support users accessing the application from geographically 
distributed locations via the internet, while application servers and databases shall be 
hosted on a centralized cloud or on -premises infrastructure.  
OE-4: The system shall rely on a stable internet connection for interaction with external 
APIs and third -party services. In case of temporary unavailability, the system shall 
continue to function using cached or fallback data where applicable.  
OE-5: All communication between client and server shall be secured using HTTPS with 
TLS 1.2 or higher to ensure data confidentiality and integrity.

CON -1: The frontend shall be implemented using Next.js (App Router) and React. Tailwind 
CSS shall be used for all styling. No alternative frontend frameworks shall be used.  
CON -2: The backend shall be implemented in Python using the FastAPI framework with typed 
models defined using Pydantic.

2.7  Non -Functional Requirements  
The following non -functional requirements define the quality attributes that VentureFit must 
satisfy in addition to its functional behaviour.  
2.7.1  Reliability  
REL-1: The system shall achieve a minimum uptime of 99.5% over any rolling 30 -day period, 
excluding scheduled maintenance of up to two hours per month, communicated at least 24 
hours in advance.  
REL-2: The background analysis pipeline shall tolerate failure of any single external data 
source, including Reddit API, Hacker News Algolia, GitHub Issues API, or LLM API, without 
halting execution; FAISS offline retrieval shall serve as the fallback for live API failures, and 
the DeepSeek → Groq → HuggingFace → deterministic template chain shall handle LLM 
outages automatically.  
REL-3: All pipeline state shall be persisted to the PostgreSQL database before and after each

3.1  System Architecture Overview  
The system follows a modular client -server architecture where the frontend, backend services, 
database layer, and external processing components are clearly separated. The architecture is 
designed to support scalable idea analysis, real -time processing of user inputs, and intelligent 
problem discovery using AI -based retrieval and clustering techniques.  
The system is divided into four main layers: the presentation layer, application layer, 
processing layer, and data layer. Each layer communicates through well -defined APIs and 
service interfaces to ensure separation of concerns, scalability, and maintainab ility. 
The frontend is responsible for user interaction and is implemented using Next.js. It 
communicates with backend APIs to handle authentication, project creation, and analysis 
visualization. The backend is built using FastAPI, which exposes RESTful endpoints  for all 
system operations. These APIs handle request validation, business logic execution, and 
coordination with processing services.  
The processing layer is the core intelligence component of the system. It includes modules for 
problem discovery, semantic search, clustering, scoring, and idea validation. This layer uses 
sentence -transformer embeddings for vector representation of text, FAISS for similarity search, 
and scikit -learn -based clustering algorithms for grouping related problem statements. Custom 
NLP filters are used to extract complaint -oriented insights from raw textual data.  
The data layer consists of PostgreSQL, which stores persistent system data such as users, 
projects, keywords, problem topics, and validation results. In addition, a vector corpus is 
maintained for FAISS -based retrieval to enable semantic search over large -scale embeddings.  
Security is enforced at multiple levels. Authentication is handled using JWT tokens stored in 
HTTP -only cookies. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt to prevent plaintext storage. Input 
validation is enforced using Pydantic schemas, and API access is protecte d using CORS 
policies.  
External integrations include Reddit, Hacker News, and GitHub APIs for real -time data 
collection, along with optional LLM services such as DeepSeek, Groq, and HuggingFace for 
query expansion and text refinement.

3.3 Architecture Style / Pattern  
The VentureFit system follows a Layered Architecture Pattern , which separates the system 
into distinct layers based on responsibility. This architecture was selected to ensure 
modularity, maintainability, scalability, and clear separation of concerns between user

1. Presentation Layer  
This layer consists of the Next.js frontend application. It is responsible for user interaction, 
including project creation, authentication, dashboard visualization, and display of analytical 
results. It communicates with the backend through REST APIs.  
2. Application Layer (API Layer)

authentication using JWT, and coordination between frontend and backend services. It acts as 
the bridge between the user interface and business logic.

outputs.  
Justification for Layered Architecture

 Chapter 4: Implementation  
This chapter discusses the implementation details of the project. It documents the core module 
functionalities using pseudocode, API integration details, user interface descriptions, and 
system deployment information. The implementation covers Modules 1 –3 only: user 
onboarding, problem discovery, and idea validation.  
4.1 Project Methodology & Algorithms  
4.1.1 Project Methodology (Step -by-Step Approach)  
1. User authentication is implemented using registration, login, logout, and JWT -based 
authentication. Passwords are securely hashed using bcrypt, and sessions are 
maintained using HTTP -only cookies.  
2. Project intake is handled through a Next.js frontend form connected to FastAPI backend 
APIs. Users submit title, description, industry, keywords, and retrieval mode.  
3. On project creation, the backend stores project metadata and keywords in PostgreSQL 
and triggers a background task for analysis.  
4. The system supports three retrieval modes: hybrid, faiss_only, and online_only.  
5. FAISS -based retrieval uses sentence -transformer embeddings to perform semantic 
search over the stored vector database.  
6. Online retrieval collects real -world data from Reddit, Hacker News, and GitHub APIs.  
7. Retrieved documents are merged and processed through preprocessing and cleaning 
pipelines.  
8. Complaint extraction is performed using rule -based NLP heuristics to identify pain -
point statements.  
9. Cleaned complaints are embedded and clustered using HDBSCAN, with fallback to 
Agglomerative and KMeans clustering.  
10. Clusters are scored based on volume (70%), engagement (20%), and heuristic pain 
severity (10%), with the final score clamped to [0, 95].  
11. Problem topics are stored in PostgreSQL and passed to the Idea Validation module.  
12. Idea validation computes cosine similarity between project and topics and generates 
final metrics such as market signal, demand, uniqueness, and recommendations.

 Chapter 5: Testing and Evaluation  
Once the system has been successfully developed, testing has to be conducted to ensure that 
the system works as intended. This is also to check that the system meets the requirements 
stated earlier. Besides that, system testing will help in finding the err ors that may be hidden 
from the user. The testing must be completed before it is deployed for use.  
There are few types of testing which include unit testing, functional testing, business rules 
testing and integration testing.  
5.1 Unit Testing

functions, methods, or classes) in isolation. The purpose is to ensure that each unit performs as 
expected, independent of the full system.

5.4 Integration Testing  
Integration testing verifies that different modules of the system work together correctly. Unlike 
unit testing (which checks isolated functions) and functional testing (which checks features 
from a user's perspective), integration testing focuses on the interfaces, linkages, and data 
flow between modules developed by different team members.  
Since FYPs are team -based, integration testing is essential  to ensure that the combined work 
of individual members forms a functioning system. Students must design at least one or two 
integration scenarios to demonstrate how modules interact and exchange data.
